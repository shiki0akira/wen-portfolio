## Overview

BigGo PMS is a price monitoring system for brands and retailers. It tracks how much products sell for across major e-commerce platforms and finds competitors' matching products to compare.

## The problem

- **Prices change every day**: to know what their products sell for on each platform, brands had to check one by one.
- **Too many products to compare**: with thousands of SKUs, finding each one's match or competitor on other platforms is nearly impossible by hand.
- **Problems found too late**: by the time a reseller's price drop was noticed, days had often passed.

## How companies use it

1. Choose the products to monitor
2. The system uses BigGo's AI search to find matching and competing products on each platform
3. Follow price changes on charts and compare against competitors
4. Schedule reports to arrive regularly, or export them anytime

## The product

(See the dashboard at the top.) The home screen shows how many products are monitored, how many reports have been sent and to whom, with products organised into groups on the left.

## Highlights

- **AI finds competitors**: even with large catalogues, BigGo's AI search matches competing products, with no manual pairing
- **Price comparison**: your products and competitors side by side, so price gaps are obvious
- **Trends at a glance**: charts show which platform or product has an unusual price
- **Export and scheduling**: export reports anytime, or have them sent automatically

## Why companies choose PMS

With a large catalogue, the slowest part is working out which product is the competitor. PMS uses BigGo's AI search to match competing products across huge catalogues and compare prices, something manual work and ordinary price trackers struggle with.
