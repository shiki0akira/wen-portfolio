## Overview

AI Hedge.finance is a crypto investment platform that uses machine learning and financial models to find trading opportunities. After connecting a wallet, users deposit assets and choose an investment strategy.

## The problem

- **Unfamiliar steps**: connecting a wallet, swapping tokens and depositing assets are new to most people, and one misunderstood step can lead to a mistake.
- **No room for error**: every step involves real money, so users need to know exactly what they're doing.

## Design highlights

### Getting the flow right first

From sign-in and wallet connection to deposits and investing, the full user flow was mapped first, deciding what each step needs before drawing any screens.

![AI Hedge.finance user flow](/images/aihedge/dapp-userflow.webp)

### A design system

Colours, type and components were defined once and shared by the web and mobile apps.

![AI Hedge.finance design system](/images/aihedge/dapp-design-system.webp)

### Confirm every important action

Before swapping, depositing, investing or redeeming, a confirmation dialog explains what will happen, so users don't tap the wrong thing.
