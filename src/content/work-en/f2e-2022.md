## Overview

THE F2E is a hackathon for front-end engineers and UI designers. The team, front-end engineers plus a UI designer, took part after work. Over three weeks, **each week meant shipping a complete project**, from design to launch.

## Three projects

### Week 1: Event website

Two characters tell the story: a front-end alien who grows octopus arms to type faster, and a UI ghost who puts on an oxygen mask to float off in search of inspiration, just like everyone growing through the challenge.

![THE F2E event website on desktop and mobile](/images/f2e/f2e-week1.webp)

### Week 2: Online signing tool (shortlisted)

"Just Sign!", an online signing tool for people who need to sign documents fast, on desktop, tablet or phone.

![Screens from the Just Sign! signing tool](/images/f2e/f2e-week2.webp)

### Week 3: Scrum Village (shortlisted)

Teaches Scrum to beginners through the metaphor of making burgers. Complete each task to learn the Scrum process and earn the Burger Chef badge.

![Scrum Village opening screen](/images/f2e/f2e-week3.webp)

## Results

Three projects in three weeks, **two shortlisted**, and the team earned a gold badge.
